# Signature list in the SEI

Feature for the Director-General's office (v0.2.0 to v0.5.0, 12/11/2026): paste a list of processes and documents and open them one at a time to sign or acknowledge ("dar ciência"). Plan in `specs/feitas/lista-assinaturas.md`; code in `comum/lista-assinaturas.js` and `sites/sei/lista-assinaturas*.js`, `sites/sei/assinar-e-proximo.js`.

- **Where to paste:** in the panel on the SEI page, via the "Colar lista" button, which opens a text box.
- **Accepted formats (one item per line):** process + document + comment (`23236.022730/2026-41 - 3383754 edital e todos os anexos`); process only (with or without comment); SEI document number only (with or without comment); columns pasted from a spreadsheet (tab); separators ` - `, `;`, `,` or just a space. Blank lines are ignored.
- **Document range (v0.9.2):** `processo - 3383754-3383903`, `3383754 - 3383903` or `3383754 a 3383903` (reversed numbers are fine). Not every number in between belongs to that process, so when the process opens the panel reads its tree and replaces the line with one item per document of that process inside the range (lowest to highest, same comment). With a process it opens the process; without one it opens the first document (which opens its process). Nothing found: warning and "Conferir de novo" (e.g. after opening closed folders in the tree). Process mask `NNNNN.NNNNNN/AAAA-DD`; the document is the SEI number (digits only, currently 7).
- **Unrecognized line:** before starting, the panel shows those lines in red; the user fixes them or starts with only the good ones.
- **Storage:** in the browser (`storage.local`, key `listaAssinaturas`) until the user clicks "Limpar lista": items, current position, done items, panel position, and the pending sign/acknowledge (`pendente`). Survives closing Firefox. Nothing goes to the internet.
- **Opening:** in the same tab, via the SEI quick search (search box at the top). With a document number it opens the document; without it, the process.
- **Navigation:** "Anterior" and "Próximo" move without marking. Counter "8/28 · 7 feitos".
- **Sign and next (v0.5.0, replaces "Feito e próximo" and the separate "Assinar"/"Dar ciência" buttons of v0.4.0):**
  - Item with a document: "Assinar e próximo" clicks "Assinar Documento" in the SEI. In the signing window, if Firefox has already filled in the saved password (and the job title is selected), the extension clicks "Assinar" by itself, once; otherwise the cursor goes to the password so the person types it and presses Enter. The click on "Assinar e próximo" is the user's confirmation for that document.
  - Acknowledgement item: "Dar ciência e próximo" clicks "Ciência" in the SEI; the person confirms in the SEI's OK dialog.
  - When the SEI reloads the tree or the document after sending, the item is marked done and the next pending one opens. Wrong password or closed window: the panel keeps waiting (up to 3 minutes; "Cancelar a espera" link).
  - Item with no document and no acknowledgement: "✓ Feito e próximo" (only opens the process). Link "Marcar feito e ir ao próximo" to skip manually.
  - **The extension never reads or stores the password**; it only checks whether the field is filled. Passwords stay in the Firefox password manager. Tip to show only the SEI password: `publicacao.md`, "Saved passwords in the SEI".
- **Full list:** the panel opens the whole list with each item's status (done/pending); one click goes to any item.
- **Highlighted comment:** the line's comment is clearly shown; an item whose comment contains "ciência" or "ciencia" is an **acknowledgement** item, shown in blue ("Só dar ciência") so it is not signed by mistake.
- **Panel:** floating, draggable (position saved), can be minimized to a bar with the counter. Shortcuts: Alt+→ (next), Alt+← (previous); without a saved list, Alt+← keeps being the browser's Back.
- **Default position:** top right of the SEI page, at the height of the process button bar and to its right, without covering the buttons (the bar `#divArvoreAcoes` is inside the right-hand frame `#divIframeVisualizacao`). On pages without an open process, the same corner just below the SEI top bar. "Lugar padrão" link.
- **When it shows:** with no list, only a small "Lista de assinaturas" button in a corner; with a list, the panel (open or minimized) on every SEI page. Only on full SEI pages (with the unit field `#lnkInfraUnidade`).
- **Who it is for:** the Director-General's office (SEI unit `PAL/REI/IFTO`, title "Direção-geral do Campus Palmas"). The extension reads the open unit on every page, because the user may switch units mid-work.
- **Settings:** option "Lista de assinaturas no SEI" with three choices: **Automática** (default: on only when the open unit is `PAL/REI/IFTO`), **Sempre ligada** (any unit) and **Desligada**. In other units, the feature only shows if the user picks "Sempre ligada". Off or another unit: button and panel disappear, but the list stays saved. Every choice is saved immediately in the browser.
- **Profiles in the future:** when there are standard profiles (Director-General, Contracts, Procurement…; idea in `melhorias-futuras.md`), "Automática" will follow the unit's profile.
